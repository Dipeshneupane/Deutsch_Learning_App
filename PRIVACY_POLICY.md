# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Deutsch Starter is a beginner-friendly German vocabulary flashcard and grammar quiz app. Version 1 is designed to work without account creation or cloud sync, and most learning progress is stored locally on the current device or browser.

## Information Stored Locally

The app stores learning data locally using device or browser storage through `shared_preferences`. This may include:

- learned vocabulary IDs
- favorite vocabulary IDs
- quiz scores by topic
- wrong-answer review data
- XP points
- streak information
- daily challenge completion
- review scheduling data
- theme preference

## No Login or Account

Version 1 does not require account registration or login. The app does not create a personal account for you inside the product.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

The app is prepared for Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, but telemetry is only active when the app owner enables Firebase in a configured environment. If enabled later, anonymized usage events and technical crash information may be collected to improve reliability and understand product usage.

## Advertising

The current build uses placeholder or test advertising integrations only. If real advertising is enabled later, third-party advertising providers such as AdMob or AdSense may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

## API Requests and Server Logs

The app requests vocabulary and grammar content from a backend API. Like most online services, the backend may record standard technical logs such as IP address, request path, timestamps, and response status for operations, monitoring, and security.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell local study-progress data. Because Version 1 stores progress locally by design, personal study progress is not shared with other users through the app.

## Your Choices

You can reset local progress from the Settings screen at any time. On web, clearing browser storage may remove saved data. On mobile, uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored progress.

## Changes

This policy should be updated before launch whenever product behavior changes, especially if accounts, cloud sync, real ads, payments, or additional analytics are introduced.
